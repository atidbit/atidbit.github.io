Saturday, Nov 28, 2026

Reuters reported that U.S. chip rules and anti-distillation safeguards are slowing China's Z.AI and Moonshot AI, Japan outlined penalties in a draft overhaul of its AI law, and Elon Musk said Google wants to rent more computing capacity from SpaceX.

Business & Funding

  • Business CNBC (Reuters)

    Musk says Google wants to rent more computing capacity from SpaceX

    Musk gave no details in his post on X. Google already pays SpaceX $920 million a month through June 2029 for capacity that includes about 110,000 Nvidia GPUs.
  • Business AI News
    Business Government of Canada

    Canada adds $700 million to its AI Compute Access Fund

    The expansion brings the fund to $1 billion to help Canadian companies cover AI computing costs, with new calls for proposals and more incentives for sovereign compute.

Policy & Safety

  • Policy Reuters (via Yahoo Finance)

    U.S. chip curbs and distillation safeguards slow China's top AI labs

    Sources say Z.AI's roughly 3-trillion-parameter GLM-5.5 has slipped, and Moonshot AI's gap between major releases could double to a year if it loses overseas compute.
  • Policy Nippon.com (Jiji Press)

    Japan outlines penalties in a draft amendment to its AI law

    A government AI panel's outline would strengthen state authority and require providers of advanced AI services to take steps to prevent cyberattacks.
  • Policy Tom's Hardware

    Banned Nvidia Blackwell racks surface at a Shanghai data center

    Export-controlled Blackwell hardware turned up at a site tied to a roughly $14 million loan, raising new questions about how restricted AI chips reach China.
  • Policy ANTARA News

    Indonesia prepares three measures in response to U.S. AI chip export rules

    The digital affairs minister said covered AI chips and servers can still be exported with case-by-case licenses, and most public AI services fall outside the rule.
  • Policy OPB

    Portland weighs a yearlong pause on new city AI software

    Council President Jamie Dunphy wants time to set up a data privacy office and AI guardrails; the City Council will discuss the proposal Dec. 2.

Research

  • Research Malspark

    Researchers find five malicious GGUF models on Hugging Face

    A scan of every public Hugging Face model repo found poisoned chat templates and hidden malware in model files, with 2,428 downloads; an open-source scanner detects them.
